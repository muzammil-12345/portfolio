Style note

Space Grotesk for headings, JetBrains Mono for labels and code, on #13151a with #e9e6df text, #c69a4e brass as the main color and #4d726b deep teal as the one accent.
The mood is a quiet, warm workroom rather than a screen glow, solid colors instead of gradients, so it reads as made by hand and stays out of the work's way.
